Travel Prepared

Tamhini Travel Tips & Safety

A few friendly suggestions to ensure your Sahyadri road trip is safe, enjoyable and stress-free.

1

Drive Carefully in Heavy Fog

Monsoon clouds can reduce visibility significantly. Drive with low beams or fog lights on, maintain moderate speeds, and sound horns at blind ghat curves.

2

Respect Waterfall Safety

Never step into fast-flowing unknown water pools or stand too close to steep slippery rocks. Water levels in mountain streams can rise quickly during heavy downpours.

3

Download Offline Route Maps

While connectivity exists along main stretches, deep valley pockets occasionally experience network drops. Downloading Google Maps offline for Pune - Mulshi - Tamhini is recommended.

4

Keep the Ghats Clean

Please carry trash bags in your car or bike and dispose of plastic, bottles, and food packets responsibly to help maintain Tamhini’s pristine natural ecosystem.
