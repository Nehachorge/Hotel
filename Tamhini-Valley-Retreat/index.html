Helpful suggestions to make your journey to चोरगे निसर्ग निवास smooth and pleasant.

  • ✔ Monsoon Travel: Carry appropriate rainwear, water-resistant footwear, and keep headlights on during foggy ghat sections.
  • ✔ Connectivity: Mobile networks can vary through valley stretches. We recommend downloading offline maps beforehand.
  • ✔ Advance Enquiry: Tamhini experiences high weekend influx during monsoon months; booking your stay in advance via WhatsApp is recommended.
