Stay Policies & Guidelines

Check-in & Check-out

Standard check-in and check-out timings will be confirmed directly during your WhatsApp reservation conversation based on your travel schedule.

Government ID Verification

As per standard hospitality norms, all adult guests must present a valid government-issued photo ID (Aadhaar, Passport, Driving License, or Voter ID) at check-in.

Eco-Conscious Nature Living

Tamhini Valley Retreat is situated in an ecologically sensitive Sahyadri region. We request all guests to avoid littering plastic in the valley and help preserve the quiet mountain atmosphere after 10:00 PM.

Meal Arrangements

Fresh, home-cooked regional meals and snacks are served at our on-site restaurant. Advance notice for lunch or dinner orders helps our kitchen team source fresh local produce.
