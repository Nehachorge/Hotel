How To Reach

Route Guidance

Nive is conveniently situated along the route to Tamhini Ghat. Here are recommended travel paths:

From Pune (Approx. 2 – 2.5 hours):

Drive from Chandani Chowk onto Paud Road → Pirangut → Paud → Mulshi Dam road → proceed ahead towards Nive in Tamhini. The entire route is paved and scenic.

From Mumbai / Navi Mumbai (Approx. 3.5 – 4.5 hours):

Route A: Via Mumbai-Pune Expressway to Khopoli exit → Pali → Vile → ascend Tamhini Ghat to Nive.
Route B: Via NH 66 to Kolad/Mangaon → climb Tamhini Ghat pass directly to Nive.

Note: We recommend calling us when passing Paud (from Pune) or Kolad/Pali (from Mumbai) so our team can guide you and keep hot food ready on your arrival!
