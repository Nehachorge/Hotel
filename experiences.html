Nature & Rejuvenation

Experiences in the Sahyadris

Rain, mist, waterfalls and lush green Sahyadris create a beautiful escape for travellers looking to slow down and reconnect with nature.

Nature's Playground

Immerse Yourself in Tamhini

From exhilarating rainy road trips to tranquil village walks and fireside evenings, life at चोरगे निसर्ग निवास moves at nature’s gentle pace.

Waterfalls

Witness dozens of seasonal streams and powerful mountain waterfalls cascading down the emerald ghat rocks.

Mountain Views

Endless rolling hills covered in vibrant moss, lush forest canopy, and swirling white clouds right outside your window.

Rainy Drives

Iconic road trips and motorcycle tours through scenic mountain passes connecting Mulshi to Konkan.

Nature Walks

Slow, peaceful village trails around Nive with fresh petrichor, wild Sahyadri flora, and cheerful birdsong.

Photography

Capture dramatic monsoon skies, misty ridgelines, morning dew on leaves, and authentic village architecture.

Bonfire Evenings

Unwind around an outdoor fire on cool mountain evenings with friends and family, sharing stories over warm tea.

Made For You
