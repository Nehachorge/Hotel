Warm Hospitality

Flavours of Maharashtra

Enjoy authentic Maharashtrian favourites and familiar Indian comfort food in a relaxed nature-inspired setting.

Home-Cooked Simplicity

Wholesome Food, Sahyadri Warmth

Food in the mountains tastes best when it is hot, fresh, and made with regional honesty. At चोरगे निसर्ग निवास, our kitchen serves hearty meals designed to fuel road trippers, comfort rainy day wanderers, and bring families together around wholesome thalis.

Whether you are returning from a trek to Savlya Ghat or pulling over during a scenic monsoon drive across Tamhini, you can always count on steaming cups of ginger chai and piping-hot local preparations.

Sample Selections

Our Culinary Highlights

Explore the flavours and regional classics commonly prepared in our kitchen.

*Seasonal Menu Notice: These are sample regional highlights representing our kitchen style. Dishes rotate based on seasonal ingredients and daily fresh market arrivals. Please check with our team on arrival for today's daily menu.

Morning Favourites

Breakfast

Warm, energizing morning classics to kickstart your day of mountain exploring and scenic drives.

  • Poha with fresh grated coconut & lemon
  • Spicy Kolhapuri/Puneri style Misal Pav with farsan
  • Crispy multigrain Thalipeeth served with fresh curd / white butter
  • Piping hot Masala Tea & Fresh Coffee
Regional Staples

Maharashtrian Specialities

Time-honoured village delicacies made using rustic spices, clay tava cooking, and authentic methods.

  • Pithla Bhakri with fiery green chilli Thecha
  • Dry Zunka Bhakri with onion & raw garlic
  • Bharli Vangi (Stuffed baby brinjals in peanut coconut gravy)
  • Varan Bhaat with dollop of pure homemade ghee
  • Cooling pink Sol Kadhi with kokum & coconut milk
Pure & Simple

Vegetarian Comforts

Simple, non-greasy home-style gravies and fresh vegetable curries that sit gently on the stomach.

  • Dal Tadka & Dal Fry with cumin tempering
  • Paneer Butter Masala / Mattar Paneer
  • Seasonal green vegetable bhaji of the day
  • Fresh soft Phulkas and hot Jowar/Bajra Bhakri
  • Steamed Basmati & Jeera Rice
Rustic Feasts

Non-Vegetarian Delights

Hearty village-style chicken curries seasoned with roasted regional spices and served with piping hot bhakri.

  • Special Regional Chicken Thali
  • Sukka Chicken with roasted masala
  • Rassa (Flavourful aromatic regional broth)
  • Egg Curry / Bhurji with hot phulkas
Afternoon Leisure

Tea & Snacks

Crispy snacks and steaming brews best enjoyed while watching the fog drift through the trees.

  • Freshly fried crisp Kanda Bhaji (Onion pakoras)
  • Batata Vada & green chutney
  • Cutting Masala Chai brewed with fresh ginger & cardamom
  • Biscuit, toast and evening snacks
Rainy Day Indulgence

Monsoon Specials

Seasonal monsoon cravings crafted especially for cold, drizzly days in the Sahyadris.

  • Garam Garam Mixed Bhaji Platter
  • Roasted corn on the cob (Bhutta) with lemon & spices
  • Steaming hot ginger-lemongrass kadha / spiced tea
  • Hot soup / Rasam style tempered broth
